The Spacewar! timeline

Scroll sideways · use ← → keys or the arrows · click a card's links to dig in · on a phone it runs top to bottom

From a hack on a new minicomputer in a Cambridge, Massachusetts laboratory to the founding myth of the video game, Spacewar! spent two decades being written, patched, carried from machine to machine, cloned, renamed, written about, and played. This timeline gathers the principal release dates, the ports and clones of its "long journey", the cultural moments, and the legacy that followed, each with a way through to the code, the photographs, and the stories on this site.
